# Project Brief

**Goal.** Win a place in MATS 12.0, Neel Nanda's mech interp stream
(Winter 2026–27). The gate is a single deliverable: ~16–20 hours of
research progress on an AI safety problem, written up, with a 1–3 page
executive summary. Due **2026-09-04 23:59 PT**.

**Success is not a big result.** His stated bar: *"If I understand what
you're claiming, what evidence you're providing, and think that evidence
supports your conclusion, that instantly puts you in the top 20% of
applicants."* One claim, defended, with the holes named.

**Two-stage program.** Exploration phase (Sept 28 – Oct 30, online, ~34
scholars, $4.2K). Research phase (Jan 19 – Apr 10 2027, Berkeley,
in-person, ~8 scholars, $19.2K + housing). Most research-phase scholars
publish co-first-author papers.

**Constraints.** 13 days elapsed. 20 counted hours plus 2 for the exec
summary. Compute is not the binding constraint (VT ARC has 514 GPUs;
everything under consideration is inference-only on a 7–32B model).
The binding constraint is hours and taste.
